📂 Zhihao Liu

Hello this is Zhihao. I did my PhD from Sugiyama-Yokoya-Ishida Lab at the University of Tokyo (東京大学), advised by Prof. Naoto Yokoya. I was also affiliated with RIKEN AIP as a junior research assistant during the same period. Prior to that, I worked at the Game AI research center of Tencent (腾讯 IEG) as a graphics engineer. My interest lies at the intersection of computer graphics, geometry modeling and 3D vision.
